Skincare Guides

Everything a beginner needs to build a routine that actually works — no jargon.

1. How to find your skin type

Wash your face, wait an hour, then notice how it feels:

  • Dry: tight, flaky, or rough.
  • Oily: shiny all over, especially by midday.
  • Combination: oily T-zone (forehead/nose) but dry cheeks.
  • Sensitive: reacts, stings, or reddens easily.
  • Normal: balanced and comfortable.

2. The correct layering order

A simple rule: apply products from thinnest to thickest.

Morning: Cleanser → Vitamin C serum → Moisturizer → Sunscreen
Night: Cleanser → Treatment (retinol/exfoliant) → Moisturizer

3. Ingredient cheat sheet

  • Vitamin C — brightening & antioxidant protection (AM).
  • Hyaluronic acid — deep hydration for all skin types.
  • Niacinamide — controls oil, minimizes pores, calms redness.
  • Retinol — smooths texture & fine lines (PM, start slow).
  • SPF — the #1 anti-aging step. Non-negotiable, every morning.

4. Beginner tips

  • Introduce one new product at a time so you can spot reactions.
  • Always patch-test new actives on your inner arm first.
  • Give products 4–6 weeks before judging results.
  • Consistency beats complexity — a simple routine you'll actually do wins.
