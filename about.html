About Seoul Glow

Honest Korean skincare reviews from people who are genuinely obsessed with glass skin.

Why we started

Korean skincare changed our skin — but getting into it was overwhelming. Ten-step routines, unfamiliar ingredients, labels we couldn't read, and hundreds of products all promising the same glow. We started Seoul Glow to be the site we wish we'd had: a simple, honest guide to K-beauty that cuts through the noise.

How we review

We don't just copy marketing claims. We actually use products, over weeks, and report what genuinely worked — and what didn't. Our promise:

  • Honest first. If a product is overhyped, we'll say so. A brand can never pay for a positive review.
  • Beginner-friendly. We explain the "why" behind every step, in plain language.
  • Skin-type aware. There's no single best product — we tell you who each pick is right for.
