The Korean Glass-Skin Routine

The famous routine, streamlined to what actually matters. Hydrate in thin layers — that's the secret to dewy, glass-like skin.

1

Oil Cleanse

Melt away sunscreen and makeup with an oil cleanser — the first half of the double cleanse.

2

Water Cleanse

Follow with a gentle low-pH gel cleanser to remove sweat and grime.

3

Tone

Rebalance and prep skin with a hydrating toner — pat it in, don't wipe.

4

Essence

The heart of K-beauty: lightweight hydration that plumps skin from within.

5

Serum / Ampoule

Target your concern — glow, soothing, or firming — with a concentrated treatment.

6

Moisturize

Seal everything in with a cream (or a sleeping mask at night).

7

Protect (SPF)

Every morning. Korean sunscreens are famously lightweight and dewy — no white cast.
