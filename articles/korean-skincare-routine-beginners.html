The 7-Step Korean Skincare Routine for Beginners

The famous routine, demystified — without buying ten products on day one.

By the Seoul Glow team · 7 min read · Updated 2026

The "10-step Korean routine" sounds intimidating, but here's the secret: it's not about doing all ten steps every day. It's about layering lightweight hydration so your skin stays plump, calm, and glowing. Below is the streamlined 7-step version we actually recommend for beginners — plus how to start with just four.

Start-simple tip: If you're brand new, begin with just Steps 2, 4, 6, and 7 (cleanser, essence, moisturizer, sunscreen). Add the rest once that feels like a habit.

Step 1 — Oil cleanse

At night, massage an oil cleanser onto dry skin to melt away sunscreen, makeup, and excess oil. This is the first half of the famous "double cleanse." Oil dissolves oil, so it lifts everything a water cleanser can't.

Step 2 — Water cleanse

Follow with a gentle, low-pH gel or foam cleanser to wash away sweat and grime. Low-pH matters: it cleans without stripping your skin barrier, so you're left soft instead of tight.

Step 3 — Tone

A hydrating toner rebalances your skin and preps it to absorb everything that follows. Pat it in with your hands — don't wipe it off with a cotton pad.

Step 4 — Essence

This is the heart of K-beauty. An essence is a lightweight, watery layer of concentrated hydration that gives skin that plump, dewy bounce. If you try one "extra" step, make it this one.

Step 5 — Serum / ampoule

Now target your specific concern with a concentrated treatment: cica for redness, hyaluronic acid for extra plump, ginseng or propolis for firming and glow. Just pick one to start.

Step 6 — Moisturize

Seal all that hydration in with a moisturizer. At night, you can swap in a sleeping mask once or twice a week for an extra glow boost.

Step 7 — Protect (SPF)

Every single morning, finish with SPF 30+. This is the most important anti-aging step there is — and Korean sunscreens are famous for being lightweight and dewy with no white cast, so you'll actually enjoy wearing it.

Putting it together

Morning: Water cleanse → Toner → Essence → Serum → Moisturizer → Sunscreen
Night: Oil cleanse → Water cleanse → Toner → Essence → Serum → Moisturizer

Be consistent for 4–6 weeks before judging results, and introduce new products one at a time. That's the whole secret to glass skin — patience and hydration.
